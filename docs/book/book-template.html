Chapter One · Welcome

Welcome, Heroes!

It is Christmas Eve at the North Pole. The sleigh is packed, the reindeer are stamping in the snow — and then every light goes dark. Grumbleweed, the Winter Troll, has snatched the Star from the top of the great tree. Without it, the sleigh cannot find its way through the storm.

Worse still, his mischievous Grumplings have scattered the night's presents across the land: in the workshop, on the frozen lake, among the rooftops.

You and your friends are the only ones who can set it right. Find the six lost presents, bring back the Star, and do it all before Midnight.

One Team

In Sleighbound you never play against each other. The whole table wins or loses together. Nobody is ever knocked out of the game, and every ending is a happy one — some are simply happier than others.

“Bring cocoa. Bring friends. Bring the Star home.”

Chapter One · Welcome

What You Need

  • The Sleighbound table open in a browser — phone, tablet or laptop.
  • One Guide: the storyteller who plays the foes and describes the world. A grown-up usually starts; older kids make wonderful co-Guides.
  • One to seven Heroes: everyone else.
  • Thirty to forty-five minutes, and something warm to drink.

The app rolls the dice, keeps the meters and moves the miniatures. If you would rather play at a real table, fetch two six-sided dice and a handful of tokens.

For the Guide

Your job is to make the world feel alive and to say “yes, and…” as often as you can. Describe what the heroes see, voice the characters they meet, and ask for a roll only when something is truly uncertain.

A Night in Four Scenes

An adventure has three or four short scenes of about ten minutes each: exploring, a tricky challenge, and a showdown at the end.

Chapter Three · Making Your Hero

Making Your Hero

A hero takes about five minutes to make. You need three things: a role, your traits and a name.

Step 1 — Pick a Role

Each role has a Knack, a special trick you may use once per scene, and starts with one item. With seven roles, every hero at a full table can be different.

Chapter Three · Making Your Hero

Step 2 — Set Your Traits

Every hero has three traits:

  • Brave — jumping, climbing, facing scary things, throwing snowballs.
  • Clever — puzzles, tricks, building, spotting things, outsmarting foes.
  • Kind — calming, helping, cheering up, making friends, even with a grumpy troll.

Split 6 points between them, each trait being 1, 2 or 3. A balanced hero is 2 / 2 / 2; a daring one might be 3 / 2 / 1.

Step 3 — Name Your Hero

Give your hero a name and one fun detail. Details like these give the Guide wonderful ideas:

“Pip Crumbleton, a gingerbread scout who is scared of milk.”
“Comet, the reindeer who can't stop sneezing.”

Your Hero Sheet

Cheer 3 — your energy. At 0 you are Tuckered Out.
Pocket 3 — you can carry up to three items.
Knack — ready again at the start of every scene.

Chapter Four · How to Play

How to Play

A Round

  1. Heroes act, in any order the team likes.
  2. Foes act — the Guide moves them.
  3. Flip a Blizzard card: a gust of wind, a surprise cocoa stand, a sneaky ambush…
  4. Midnight ticks one step closer.

Your Turn

Move up to 4 squares and take one action:

  • Try something tricky (roll the dice)
  • Use an item
  • Help a friend
  • Wake up a Tuckered Out friend beside you
  • Pick up, open or talk to something

Scenes

Explore the map and meet characters. Beat a Challenge such as crossing the cracking ice. Win the Showdown against the foes.

Chapter Four · How to Play

Rolling the Dice

When you try something tricky, the Guide names the trait that fits. Roll two dice and add that trait.

You getWhat happens
9 or moreYou did it!
6 to 8You did it… but something small goes wrong too.
5 or lessUh-oh! It doesn't work. The team gains a Twinkle.

Not sure which trait? Say how you do it. Sneaking past with a joke is Clever; leaping over is Brave; offering a cookie is Kind.

9
Chapter Four · How to Play

Helping a Friend

Before someone rolls, say how you help: “I hold the ladder steady!” They get +1. Up to two friends may help at once, and helping does not use your turn.

Twinkles

Twinkles are magic sparkles the whole team shares. You start with one per hero and gain another every time a roll goes wrong, so even bad luck helps. Spend one to:

  • roll again,
  • add +2 to a roll, or
  • cancel the “something goes wrong” part of a 6–8.

Cheer & Tuckered Out

Coal, snowballs and slippery falls cost Cheer. At 0 Cheer you are Tuckered Out: your mini lies down for a nap and you cannot act. You are not out of the game! A friend beside you can spend their action to wake you with 1 Cheer.

Hot Cocoa, Cookies and the Carol Singer's song all bring Cheer back.

Chapter Five · Foes

Foes

In Sleighbound you never hurt anyone. Foes have Grump instead of health. Each success against a foe takes away 1 Grump, or 2 on a 9+ using its weak trait. At 0 Grump it is calmed, outsmarted or tagged out, and waddles off.

FoeWhat it doesTry being…
GrumplingsGrab presents and runKind
Coal ImpsThrow coal from 3 squaresBrave
Wind-up RatsFast; always in threesClever

Snow GolemsSlow, huge, block pathsClever
GrumbleweedThe Winter Troll himself…?

Grumplings only want a hug. And Grumbleweed looks more sad than mean, doesn't he? Watch him closely…

Chapter Seven · Winning the Night

Winning the Night

Two meters sit at the top of the table:

  • Presents (0–6) — how many lost presents you have found.
  • Midnight (12 steps) — when it fills, the night is over.

Bring back the Star before Midnight and the sleigh takes off. The more presents you found, the more homes get their gifts. And if you find a kind way to deal with Grumbleweed… well, you'll see.

Table Rules for Families

  • Yes, and… — build on each other's ideas.
  • Share the spotlight. Ask quiet players what their hero does.
  • Little players just say what they want; a grown-up does the sums.
  • Table moments — between scenes, act out a gift, draw an ornament or hum a carol to win Twinkles.
  • Pause anytime. Snack breaks are part of the adventure.
