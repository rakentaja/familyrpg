# Sleighbound — Player's Handbook

*A Hearthside adventure for 2–8 players, ages 6 and up.*

![Cover](../art/handbook/cover.jpg)

## Welcome, heroes!

It's Christmas Eve at the North Pole. The sleigh is packed, the reindeer are ready — and then the lights go out. **Grumbleweed, the Winter Troll**, has snatched the Star from the top of the great tree. Without it, the sleigh can't find its way through the snow. Worse, his mischievous Grumplings have scattered the night's presents all over the land.

You and your friends are the only ones who can fix it. Find the six lost presents, bring back the Star, and do it all **before Midnight**.

In Sleighbound you work as **one team**. You win together or not at all. Nobody gets knocked out of the game, and every ending is a happy one — some are just happier than others.

## What you need

- The Sleighbound table open in your browser (phone, tablet or laptop).
- One **Guide** — the storyteller who plays the foes and describes the world. Grown-ups usually start; older kids make great co-Guides.
- 1–7 **Heroes** — everyone else.
- 30–45 minutes and something warm to drink.

The app rolls dice, tracks meters and moves the minis. If you'd rather play at a real table, grab two six-sided dice and some tokens.

## The world

![Map of the North Pole](../art/handbook/map.jpg)

- **Toy Workshop** — where every toy in the world is built. Tonight, the wind-up toys have escaped.
- **Candy Cane Forest** — tall striped trees and sticky paths. Easy to get lost.
- **Frozen Lake** — slippery! When you step on the ice you slide until you bump into something.
- **Gingerbread Village** — tiny sweet houses and very worried gingerbread folk.
- **Chimney Rooftops** — jump between roofs. Some gaps are too wide without wings or a clever plan.
- **The Troll's Ice Cave** — where Grumbleweed hides with the Star.

## Making your hero

Making a hero takes about five minutes. You need three things: a **role**, your **traits**, and a **name**.

### Step 1 — Pick a role

![The seven hero roles](../art/handbook/roles.jpg)

Each role has a **Knack**: a special trick you can use **once per scene**. Each role also starts with one item. With seven roles, every hero at a full table can be different.

| Role | Knack | Starting item |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reindeer Flyer** | *Fly* — move 8 squares over anything, carrying one friend | Harness bell |
| **Snow Sprite** | *Freeze* — turn water into ice, or stop a foe for a round | Snowflake charm |
| **Gingerbread Scout** | *Crumb trail* — jump back to any square you've visited this scene | Gumdrop |
| **Toy Soldier** | *Stand guard* — friends next to you can't lose Cheer this round | Drum |
| **Carol Singer** | *Sing* — every friend within 3 squares gets 1 Cheer back | Songbook |
| **Polar Bear Baker** | *Cookie break* — hand out 2 Cookies | Rolling pin |
| **Star Keeper** | *Shine* — reveal hidden things within 4 squares; nearby foes lose 1 Grump | Lantern |

### Step 2 — Set your traits

- **Brave** — jumping, climbing, standing up to scary things, throwing snowballs.
- **Clever** — puzzles, tricks, building, spotting things, outsmarting foes.
- **Kind** — calming, helping, cheering up, making friends — even with a grumpy troll.

You have **6 points** to split between them. Each trait must be **1, 2 or 3**. A balanced hero is 2 / 2 / 2; a daring hero might be 3 / 2 / 1.

### Step 3 — Name your hero

Give your hero a name and one fun detail. *"Pip Crumbleton, a gingerbread scout who is scared of milk."* *"Comet, the reindeer who can't stop sneezing."*

### Your hero sheet

- **Cheer: 3.** Your energy. At 0 you're **Tuckered Out**.
- **Pocket: 3 items.**
- **Knack:** ready at the start of every scene.

## How to play

### Taking turns

1. **Heroes act**, in any order the team likes.
2. **Foes act.** The Guide moves the foes.
3. **Blizzard card.** Flip one Event card.
4. **Midnight ticks** one step closer.

On your turn you can **move up to 4 squares** and do **one action**: try something tricky (roll), use an item, help a friend, revive a Tuckered Out friend next to you, or pick up / open / talk to something.

### Rolling the dice

![Dice and Twinkles](../art/handbook/dice.jpg)

Roll **two dice** and add the trait the Guide names.

| You get | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **9 or more** | You did it! |
| **6 to 8** | You did it… but something small goes wrong too. |
| **5 or less** | Uh-oh! It doesn't work. The team gains a **Twinkle**. |

Not sure which trait? Describe *how* you do it. Sneaking past with a joke is Clever; leaping over is Brave; offering a cookie is Kind.

### Helping a friend

Before someone rolls, say how you help — *"I hold the ladder steady!"* — and they get **+1**. Up to two friends can help at once. Helping doesn't use your turn.

### Twinkles

Shared by the **whole team**. Start with one per hero; gain one every time a roll goes wrong. Spend one to roll again, add **+2**, or cancel the "something goes wrong" part of a 6–8.

### Cheer and getting Tuckered Out

Coal, snowballs and slippery falls cost **Cheer**. At 0 you're **Tuckered Out**: your mini naps and you can't act — but you're not out. A friend next to you can use their action to wake you with 1 Cheer. Hot Cocoa, Cookies and the Carol Singer bring Cheer back too.

## Foes

![Grumbleweed and his crew](../art/handbook/foes.jpg)

Foes have **Grump** instead of health. Each success removes 1 Grump (2 on a 9+ with their weak trait). At 0 they're calmed, outsmarted or tagged out — and waddle off.

| Foe | What it does | Try being… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Grumplings** | Grab presents and run away | Kind — they just want a hug |
| **Coal Imps** | Throw coal from 3 squares away | Brave |
| **Wind-up Rats** | Fast, always come in threes | Clever |
| **Blizzard Wisps** | Blow heroes 2 squares away | Brave |
| **Snow Golems** | Slow, huge, block the path | Clever |
| **Grumbleweed** | The Winter Troll himself | Watch closely… |

## Items

![Items](../art/handbook/items.jpg)

- **Hot Cocoa** — get 2 Cheer back.
- **Cookie** — get 1 Cheer back, or give it to a friend next to you.
- **Snowball** — throw up to 4 squares; on 6 or more the foe loses 1 Grump.
- **Candy Cane Hook** — pull a friend, foe or present 3 squares toward you.
- **Jingle Bell** — everyone moves 2 extra squares this round.
- **Mystery Present** — draw 2 items and keep one.
- **Sled** — zoom 10 squares in a straight line, downhill only.

## Winning the night

- **Presents (0–6)** — how many lost presents you've found.
- **Midnight (12 steps)** — when it fills, the night is over.

Bring back the Star before Midnight and the sleigh takes off. The more presents you found, the more homes get their gifts. And if you find a kind way to deal with Grumbleweed… well, you'll see.

## Table rules for families

- **Yes, and…** — build on each other's ideas.
- **Everyone gets the spotlight.**
- **Little players** just say what they want to do; a grown-up handles the numbers.
- **Table moments** — act out a gift, draw an ornament or hum a tune to win Twinkles.
- **Pause anytime.** Snack breaks are part of the adventure.
